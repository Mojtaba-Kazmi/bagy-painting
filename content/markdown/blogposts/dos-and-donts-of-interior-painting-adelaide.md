## The Do’s and Don’ts of Interior Painting in Adelaide: Expert Advice

A fresh coat of paint can completely transform your home, but **getting it right** takes more than just choosing a colour and grabbing a roller. Whether you’re tackling a DIY job or hiring a professional, knowing the **do’s and don’ts of interior painting** will save you time, money, and frustration.

At **Bagy Painting**, we’ve helped countless Adelaide homeowners achieve stunning interiors. Here’s our expert advice on what to do—and what to avoid—when painting your home.

---

## ✅ The Do’s of Interior Painting

### **1. Choose the Right Paint for Adelaide’s Climate**  
Adelaide experiences **hot summers and cool winters**, so it’s essential to select the right paint that can handle temperature changes. **Low-VOC, washable paints** work best for durability and air quality.  

### **2. Prep Your Walls Properly**  
Good prep work is the difference between a professional-looking finish and a patchy job.  
- Fill in cracks and holes with **a high-quality filler**.  
- Sand down any rough spots.  
- Clean the walls with **sugar soap** to remove dust and grease.  

### **3. Use High-Quality Brushes & Rollers**  
Cheap brushes leave streaks, and low-quality rollers can shed lint into your paint. Invest in **good-quality tools** to get a smooth, even finish.

### **4. Apply a Primer When Needed**  
Skipping primer can lead to uneven absorption and dull-looking walls. If you’re painting **over a dark colour or a porous surface**, always use **a good primer** first.

### **5. Consider the Lighting in Your Home**  
Natural and artificial lighting affect how a colour looks. Test paint samples **on different walls** and check them throughout the day before committing.

---

## ❌ The Don’ts of Interior Painting

### **1. Don’t Paint Over Dirty or Damp Walls**  
Dirt, dust, and moisture can cause paint to bubble or peel. Always clean and dry walls thoroughly before painting.

### **2. Don’t Skip Painter’s Tape**  
For **crisp edges and clean lines**, use high-quality **painter’s tape** instead of masking tape. Remove it while the paint is still slightly wet to avoid peeling.

### **3. Don’t Rush the Drying Process**  
Adelaide’s weather can be unpredictable, but resist the urge to turn on fans or heaters to speed up drying. **Let each coat dry naturally** to prevent uneven patches.

### **4. Don’t Use the Wrong Finish**  
Flat paints hide imperfections but stain easily, while gloss finishes are easy to clean but highlight flaws. **Choose the right finish** for each room:  
- **Matte/flat** – Best for ceilings and low-traffic areas.  
- **Satin/eggshell** – Great for living rooms and bedrooms.  
- **Semi-gloss/gloss** – Ideal for kitchens, bathrooms, and trims.  

### **5. Don’t Forget to Ventilate the Room**  
Always keep windows open and fans running to avoid **paint fumes building up**. Even **low-VOC paints** need proper ventilation for safety.
