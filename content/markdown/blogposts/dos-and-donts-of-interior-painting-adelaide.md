## The Do’s of Interior Painting  

### **1. Choose the Right Paint for Adelaide’s Climate**  
Adelaide’s hot summers and cool winters demand paints that can handle temperature changes. **Low-VOC, washable paints** are ideal for both durability and air quality.

### **2. Prep Your Walls Properly**  
Preparation is key to a flawless finish.  
- Fill cracks and holes with a **quality filler**.  
- Sand rough areas smooth.  
- Clean walls with **sugar soap** to remove dust, grease, and stains.  

### **3. Invest in Quality Brushes and Rollers**  
Cheap tools leave streaks and lint. High-quality brushes and rollers ensure smooth, even coverage.  

### **4. Use Primer When Needed**  
If painting over **dark colours or porous surfaces**, primer helps achieve even coverage and vibrant results.  

### **5. Test Colours in Different Lighting**  
Lighting changes how paint looks. Always test samples on multiple walls and view them at different times of day before deciding.  

---

## The Don’ts of Interior Painting  

### **1. Don’t Paint Over Dirty or Damp Walls**  
Moisture and dirt can cause bubbling and peeling. Surfaces must be **clean and dry** before painting.  

### **2. Don’t Skip Painter’s Tape**  
For **crisp edges**, use painter’s tape. Remove it while the paint is slightly wet to prevent peeling.  

### **3. Don’t Rush the Drying Process**  
Let each coat dry naturally. Using heaters or fans can cause uneven drying and patchy results.  

### **4. Don’t Choose the Wrong Finish**  
Each finish has its purpose:  
- **Flat/matte** – Best for ceilings and low-traffic rooms.  
- **Satin/eggshell** – Ideal for living rooms and bedrooms.  
- **Semi-gloss/gloss** – Perfect for kitchens, bathrooms, and trims.

### **5. Don’t Forget Ventilation**  
Even **low-VOC paints** need airflow. Keep windows open to avoid fumes and speed up safe drying.

## FAQs About Interior Painting in Adelaide  

**1. How often should I repaint my interior walls?**  
Most interiors need repainting every 5–7 years, though high-traffic areas may need it sooner.  

**2. Which paint finish is easiest to clean?**  
**Satin or semi-gloss** finishes are best for easy cleaning, especially in kitchens and bathrooms.  

**3. Can I stay home while painting is being done?**  
Yes, but proper ventilation is important. Using **low-VOC paints** helps keep fumes to a minimum.
